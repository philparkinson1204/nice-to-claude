Three cards and a one-page summary, made on your computer from your Claude Code history. Nothing leaves this page unless you post it.

Download a card, then use a post link: it opens a draft with the caption filled in, and you attach the card. LinkedIn only takes the link, so the caption is copied for you to paste.
